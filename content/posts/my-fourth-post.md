In unit 4, we spent a lot of time and looked around on the tools Metabase and Chatbot, which made this unit more engaging and practical. While exploring both of these two tools, it’s kind of exciting and also challenging as I don’t have any experience in working with these kinds of tools.

In the beginning, as I don’t have any experience with these tools I feel more challenging. There is also a need for patience, to learn how to organise data properly, creating chatbot’s responses and making dashboards and graphs.  The inaccurate responses from chatbot, confusion and getting clarity in making charts are movements which made me more struggling. But from these things, I found that in the learning process making mistakes is also a part of it.

Another significant thing I get from this unit is, through the experimentations the practical learning became easier sometimes. I learned that, in learning, watching only tutorials results less than needed. But practicing and playing through the tools, learning from already experienced people or classmates helped to learn in an easier way. Through this unit, I understand that even difficult tools also become easier with the help of teamwork and practice which help me to improve my confidence level on computing.

According to me, understanding the way technology is designed in user interaction is one of the important learnings from this unit. The user experience played a significant role in both the chatbot responses and data visualizations.

Overall, through this unit 4, I got more understanding and practical thinking about the technology, my patience got developed and my problem solving skills also got improved. I get an idea about how effective practical learning is and understand the difference between simply learning through theory and experimenting practically which helps us to learn more and better.
