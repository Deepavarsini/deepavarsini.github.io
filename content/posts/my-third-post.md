Learning about failure sounds very controversial and has different perspectives among everyone. In contrast to studying successful technologies which are familiar, in this unit we deeply think about the failure, how it happened and why it happened which is not usual.

From this unit, I learnt that failures are not happening because of the single mistake. Some of the small errors in designing, software or decisions made by humans are also the reasons behind the failures. In Information systems, careful planning and testing are the most important things. Sometimes mistakes can also be made by widely known organizations as well.

In failure, getting to know about the technical cause of the failure is a challenging part. In the beginning understanding the technical terms was harder for me, but I sought it with the help of my group members. In this kind of case, sharing ideas and getting to know about the hard stuff through groupmates helps to understand the hardest things in an easy way. One of my significant perspectives is, by reading Amy Edmondson’s “Right kind of wrong” I figured out one thing. Failures also help for our improvement, if we accept it and try to fix it.

Through this unit, I understand the consequences faced by the technology. I realised that, People of the world focus on trying to know how to succeed in their life, but most of the people are not thinking about why they fail, even they are not accepting their mistakes sometimes.
