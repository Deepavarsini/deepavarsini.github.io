Learning some different things in an unusual way is kind of interesting and spectacular for me, which gets my attention even though I'm not having it. I get to know about the basic difference between the data, information, knowledge and wisdom through the DIKW model in a better way. Before I went through these terms in this unit, I thought these words are almost similar. But now the difference between them and how it depends on context, meaning, and insights was quite new to me.

One of the things that I found a little difficult was understanding how different people interpret the same information in different ways. As a literature student, in literature, readers can define the meaning of the text. Literary pieces do not have a single meaning. In some ways information is kind of similar to that. The readings ( The Information: A History, a Theory, a Flood by James Gleick) made me think very deeply about how information is not always fully objective. Some areas of the same data can get different opinions, for example we can connect this with some real life stuff like news and statistics.

Overall, through this unit I understand the significance of analysing the information carefully. I also learned some basics about Zettlr, markdown, wiki, and how to write mathematical expressions in latex format. One of the most difficult but needed things is github and gitbash.
